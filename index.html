Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. Puthi is free for everyone, forever. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does it need internet?

No. Reading, highlighting, notes and exporting all work offline. Internet is only needed to download the app itself.

Where do my books and notes live?

On your phone only. Puthi keeps your library, highlights, notes and progress in the app's own storage and never uploads them.

How does Puthi find my PDFs?

You choose a folder, such as Downloads, and Puthi lists every PDF inside. You can also add single files at any time.

Can I take my notes out?

Yes. Export your highlights, notes and remarks for one book or all books as a Word document or a PDF.
